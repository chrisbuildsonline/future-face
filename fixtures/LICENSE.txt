Derived files
Every file in fixtures/demo/ is derived from that face. They are the output of
YouCam AI Aging Generator, AI Skin Simulation and AI Skin Analysis, run on the
record date given by "recordedAt" in fixtures/demo/result.json (2026-10-30),
plus resizing and re-encoding of the original image. fixtures/demo/input.jpg is
the re-encoded copy of the source face. The scores in result.json come from AI
Skin Analysis.
