# Future Face

Two faces of you at 45, side by side: the one who kept living as now, and the one who wore sunscreen every day in your city. Built from your selfie with YouCam Skin Analysis, the Aging Generator and Skin Simulation, with the gap measured, cited and shoppable.

Built for [YouCam API Skin AI & eCommerce VTO Hackathon](https://youcam-api-skin-ai-ecommerce.devpost.com/). Submissions close 2026-11-02.

## What it does

Two faces of you at 45, side by side: the one who kept living as now, and the one who wore sunscreen every day in your city. Built from your selfie with YouCam Skin Analysis, the Aging Generator and Skin Simulation, with the gap measured, cited and shoppable.

## Stack

Next.js, YouCam AI Skin Analysis (SD, skin_age + per-concern scores), AI Aging Generator (res_age frames), AI Skin Simulation (spots/wrinkle/texture intensities from cited effect sizes), Open-Meteo UV index, Playwright, recorded fixtures for the zero-key path
